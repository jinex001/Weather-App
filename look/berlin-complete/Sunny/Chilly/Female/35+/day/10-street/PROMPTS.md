# Product generation prompts

Built-in image generation. Existing editorial reused as outfit reference. One call per product.

## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Input image is the outfit reference. Create ONE standalone square product photograph of ONLY the single item category described below. Preserve visible reference color, material appearance, silhouette, seams and construction. No redesign, no invented ornament, conservative hidden construction. Warm ivory floor/background near #F3EFE8, soft studio light, subtle contact shadow, entire item centered with generous margins. Clothing neatly laid flat, camera vertically overhead. Low footwear: matching pair on soles, parallel side by side with both toes toward top, camera vertically overhead. One matching pair counts as one item. Bags and jewelry: full standalone item neatly arranged, no body. No person, skin, hands, mannequin, hanger, other item categories, text, logos, watermarks, grids, collage or multi-item board. Do not include scene props or food.
