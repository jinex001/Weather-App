# Product generation prompts

Built-in image generation. Existing editorial reused as outfit reference, not regenerated. One call per product.

## Shared prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Input image is the outfit reference. Create ONE independent square product photograph of ONLY the single item described below. Preserve visible reference color, material appearance, silhouette, seams, closures and construction. No redesign or invented ornament; reconstruct hidden construction conservatively. Warm ivory floor/background near #F3EFE8, soft studio light, subtle contact shadow, entire item centered with generous margins. Clothing: neat overhead flat lay, no body. Low footwear: matching pair on soles, parallel side by side, toes toward image top, true vertical overhead camera. ANKLE BOOTS ONLY: both boots upright on soles in neat three-quarter view showing full shafts and toes, never overhead or lying on sides. Bags and jewelry: one full standalone item neatly arranged, no body. A matching pair of earrings/socks/shoes counts as one item category. No other categories, person, skin, hands, mannequin, hanger, logos, text, watermark, grid, collage, multi-item board, food or scene props.
